### Valcon is a core mod for Starfell: RoTVK (coming soon) for Neoforge 1.21.1. This mod is not gaurenteed support to work with any other pack.

** **

Feature List:
- Lembas bread, an extremely filling new food made out of aethelas, dough, and gold.
- The soul forge, a new station capable of applying enchanted tablets and soul steel to make your equipment unbreakable.
- Bast statue, a block that provides resistance to nearby players, acting as a mini beacon.
- Star Taker Disc, made by SociopathicFungusGirl.
- Devil Trigger disc by Casey Edwards, instrumental.
- A new weapon, the Soulborn Blade.
- A few crafting materials, such as souls and enchanting guides, made to slow down progression dealing.
- A reworked frostbiter from Windswept, you can now ram dash and control them more easily.
- A new effect that makes you unable to make critical hits.
- A new material set called ashen steel, along with ashen steel handles.

This mod is still a work in progress!
